GOSHTI is a Marathi audio-story app for children aged 2–9. A parent sets it up; the child listens.
There are **no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics** in GOSHTI.

## What the app asks for
- The child's **first name** (or a nickname), **age**, and whether the child is a **boy or girl**
  (Marathi grammar needs it). A parent can use a nickname instead of a real name.
- Optionally, a **story idea** typed by the child or parent.

## What happens to it
- It is **saved on the phone** so GOSHTI can remember the child between visits.
- To make a **new story** (not the ready-made library stories), the name, age, boy/girl and idea are sent
  to our story server (hosted on Supabase) and processed by **Google Cloud (Vertex AI)** to write the story
  and to make Aaji's voice. We do not keep the child's name or idea on our server after the story is made;
  server logs hide them. Google processes them under Google Cloud's terms and does not use them to train its models.
- Ready-made library stories do not send anything about the child.
- We do **not** sell data, share it for advertising, or build profiles of children.
- GOSHTI does not use the microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location.

## Safety
- The child cannot chat with the AI. The AI is only used to write a story and read it aloud.
  Ideas are checked for safety before and after, and unsafe ideas are declined.
- If a story is ever wrong or upsetting, a parent can tell us at [support email] and we will fix it.

## Parent controls
- **Delete everything:** Parent mode (behind a parent check) → "सगळी माहिती काढून टाका" removes the child's
  name, stories, favourites and saved voices from the phone. Deleting the app does the same.
- To ask about data we may hold, write to [support email].

## Children
GOSHTI is designed for children, with a parent in control of setup and purchases. We collect only what the
app needs to tell a story. [If you are in India: we process a child's data only with a parent's consent,
given when the parent sets the app up.]

## Changes
If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for important changes, show a notice in Parent mode.
